C533: Wave Start Delay
Countdown loaded to 1A at the start of each wave by Main_Game_Loop. Checked each frame at Main_Loop_Poll; while non-zero the frame is skipped and control branches to the wave-end handler at Dispatch_Narrow_Entity_Approach.
