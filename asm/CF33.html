CF33: Dispatch Narrow Entity Approach
Used by the routine at Main_Game_Loop.
Per-frame handler for approaching narrow entities. Decrements Animation_Frame_Counter_Two and jumps to Animate_Wave_Start_Sequence when zero; otherwise resets the animation scroll position, dispatches the entity list, decrements Animation_Frame_Counter_One, and returns to the main poll loop. Called from Main_Game_Loop.
