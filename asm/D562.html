D562: Draw Playfield Border Row
Used by the routine at DrawPlayfieldBorder.
Caller supplies the row in L. Sweeps H from 06 to F9 inclusive, calling PlotCharacterCell_Draw each time to OR the border glyph across that row of the screen buffer. Used only from DrawPlayfieldBorder.
DrawPlayfieldBorderRow D562 LD H,$06 H=06 (start of the horizontal sweep).
DrawPlayfieldBorderRow_Loop D564 PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack (coordinates for PlotCharacterCell_Draw).
D565 CALL PlotCharacterCell_Draw Call PlotCharacterCell_Draw (OR-blit at this cell).
D568 POP HL Restore HL from the stack.
D569 INC H Increment H (next column in the sweep).
D56A LD A,H Loop back to DrawPlayfieldBorderRow_Loop until the full row has been drawn.
D56B CP $FA
D56D JR NZ,DrawPlayfieldBorderRow_Loop
D56F RET Return.
