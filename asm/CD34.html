CD34: Clear Colour Bar Column
Used by the routines at Handle_Homing_Enemy, Process_Entity_Record_Fast, Kill_Tracked_Enemy_Entity, Process_Cluster_Entity, Kill_Wall_Bouncing_Enemy and Process_Final_Phase_Enemy_Entity.
Clears a seven-column, six-pixel-row strip from the screen pixel area at the column position held in Colour_Bar_Column, then jumps to PrintPlayerScore to redraw the column.
Clear_Colour_Bar_Column CD34 LD B,$06 Prepare to clear six pixel rows of the colour bar column.
CD36 LD H,$42
For each row, point to the target column and zero seven pixel cells.
Clear_Colour_Bar_Column_Row CD38 LD A,($C420) Point to the colour bar column in the current pixel row.
CD3B LD L,A
CD3C XOR A Zero seven pixel cells at the column position.
CD3D LD (HL),A

CD40 INC HL

CD44 INC HL

CD49 LD (HL),A
CD4A INC H Advance to the next pixel row and repeat until all six are cleared.
CD4B DJNZ Clear_Colour_Bar_Column_Row
CD4D JP PrintPlayerScore Jump to PrintPlayerScore to redraw the score display column.
