F04D: Fill Row
Used by the routine at Draw_Colour_Ring.
Writes the current border colour to D cells starting at (H=column, L=row), advancing one column right per cell. Called by Draw_Colour_Ring to paint the top and bottom horizontal edges of each colour ring.
Input
HL Starting cell coordinate (H = column, L = row)
D Number of cells to fill

F04F CALL PlotCharacterCell_Draw
