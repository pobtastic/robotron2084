53156: Paint Narrow Entity Attributes
Used by the routines at Process_Entity_Record_Fast, Tick_Entity_Colour_Timer and Process_Cluster_Entity.
Paints the entity colour attribute across a two-cell-wide, three-row region of the attribute area at the entity's screen position. Uses Screen_Row_Address_Lookup_Table to resolve each pixel row to its attribute address; the colour is read from shadow B'. Called by Process_Entity_Record_Fast, Tick_Entity_Colour_Timer and Process_Cluster_Entity.
