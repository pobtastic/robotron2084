54553: Initialise Wave Start Sprites
Used by the routines at Main_Game_Loop and Animate_Wave_Start_Sprites.
Copies the wave sprite template at Intro_Sprite_Template to the working state at Intro_Sprite_State and resets the animation frame counter to 14. Called once per wave from Main_Game_Loop before Animate_Wave_Start_Sprites begins advancing frames.
Initialise_Wave_Start_Sprites 54553 LD HL,54529 Set up to copy the sprite template to the working state.
54556 LD DE,54541
54559 LD BC,12
54562 LDIR Copy the sprite template to the working sprite state.
54564 LD A,14 Reset the animation frame counter to 14.
54566 LD (50481),A
54569 RET Return.
