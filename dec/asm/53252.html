53252: Paint Wide Entity Attributes
Used by the routine at Process_Wall_Bouncing_Enemy.
Paints the entity colour attribute across a three-cell-wide, three-row region of the attribute area at the entity's screen position. Uses Screen_Row_Address_Lookup_Table to resolve each pixel row to its attribute address; the colour is read from shadow B'. Called by Process_Wall_Bouncing_Enemy.
