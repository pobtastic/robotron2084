54570: Animate Wave Start Sprites
Used by the routine at Animate_Wave_Start_Sequence.
Advances the wave start sprite animation by one frame. Renders each of the three sprite state entries via Draw_Clipped_Wide_Sprite, scrolls its Y-position down by 6 pixels and advances its X-position by its stored velocity. After 14 frames the closing sprite is drawn and the animation restarts via Initialise_Wave_Start_Sprites.
