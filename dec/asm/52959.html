52959: Step Narrow Entity Approach
Per-entity frame handler for an approaching narrow entity. Advances the animation phase counter, moves the entity down by six pixels per frame, and on the final frame installs Handle_Homing_Enemy as the entity's new handler. Called from Main_Game_Loop.
